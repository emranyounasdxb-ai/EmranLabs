Portfolio screen tours are edited sequences of real screenshots, with caption files beside the videos. They illustrate the interface and do not claim to record a customer transaction.

Weather forecasts come from [MET Norway](https://api.met.no/) under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), with visible attribution in the widget. Display temperatures are rounded and daily ranges are calculated from the available forecast hours. Open-Meteo's customer API can be used when a suitable private API key is configured.
